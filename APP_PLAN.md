# Healthy Together — product plan

## Screens
- Today: 8 big checkboxes, points earned today, streak-free supportive copy
- Our Progress: weekly and monthly progress, lifetime points, each person's goals
- Rewards: personal rewards and separate shared rewards
- Guides: Planet Fitness Partner Plan reference
- My Settings: profile and optional private weight log

## Eight preset habits
- Planned workout — 10 points; Emily target 2/week, David target 1/week initially
- Enjoyable movement — 5 points; 2/week each
- Stretch or mobility — 5 points; Emily 2/week, David 1/week
- Balanced meal — 5 points; 5/week each
- Personal nutrition goal — 5 points; 5/week each
- Hydration — 5 points; 5/week each
- Sleep and recovery — 5 points; 5/week each
- Healthy activity together — 5 points; 1/week each

## Rules
- 10 bonus points when at least 75% of the person's weekly targets are met.
- Monday–Sunday weeks, correctly handled across month boundaries.
- Keep monthly history and lifetime totals.
- Individual rewards cost 50, 100, 200, or 400 points.
- Shared rewards have separate progress; they never subtract personal points.
- Optional non-scale wins; an explicitly recorded optional win may earn +5.
- Weight tracking is optional, private to its owner, and never affects points.
- No calorie logging or mandatory daily journal.

## Privacy acceptance tests
- Emily can read/write her weight entries; David cannot read them.
- David can read/write his weight entries; Emily cannot read them.
- Each person can read/write only their own check-ins.
- Both can see shared household progress and shared rewards.
- Personal rewards/claims are private to their owner.
- Logged-out users and users from another household cannot access private data.
