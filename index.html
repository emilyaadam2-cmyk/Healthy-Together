DAVID'S PLAN · GYM COMPANION

Start with 5–10 minutes of easy, low-impact cardio, then use a comfortable weight that lets you keep good form. Rest as needed. These are guide cards; check off the matching quest on Today when you finish.

💪 Workout 1 · Arms + Chest

Primary workout · about 40–50 minutes including warm-up

  1. Seated chest press — 3 sets × 10–12 reps
  2. Incline dumbbell press — 3 × 10–12
  3. Cable crossover or pec deck — 3 × 12–15
  4. Cable biceps curls — 3 × 12–15
  5. Triceps pushdowns — 3 × 12–15

Move smoothly; keep shoulders comfortable and stop if you feel sharp pain.

🦵 Workout 2 · Legs + Core

Primary workout · controlled, comfortable range

  1. Seated leg press — 3 × 10–12
  2. Seated leg curl — 3 × 12–15
  3. Seated leg extension — 3 × 12–15 (optional; skip if uncomfortable)
  4. Calf raises — 3 × 15
  5. Crunches or dead bugs — 3 × 12–15
  6. Plank holds — 3 × 20–30 seconds

Use a pain-free range, avoid locking your knees, and choose the core option that feels best.

🎁 Workout 3 · Full-Body Bonus

Optional extra session · a bonus, never a requirement

  1. Lat pulldown — 3 × 10–12
  2. Seated cable row — 3 × 10–12
  3. Seated shoulder press — 3 × 10–12
  4. Goblet squat to a box or bench — 3 × 10, only if comfortable
  5. Dumbbell farmer's carries — 3 × 30 seconds

Use a stable bench/box and light load for carries. Swap or skip any move that causes knee or joint pain.

🌱 Gentle start · 10 minutes

Easy warm-up, comfortable walk or march, gentle mobility, and slow breathing.

🧘 Mobility reset · 8 minutes

Gentle ankle circles, hip shifts, supported hip-flexor stretch, chest opener, seated spinal rotations, and slow breathing. Stop any movement that causes sharp pain.
